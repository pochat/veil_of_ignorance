# DGMD-E28 Final Project
In this game, you enter a world where you don’t start with a fixed identity. Instead, you are randomly assigned a fate: **rich or poor**.
Your fate determines your starting resources, opportunities, and challenges. The game explores themes of social inequality, privilege, and the impact of circumstances on one's life trajectory.

## How It Works

- You enter your name on the welcome screen
- The game stores your name in `localStorage`
- Each question presents two choices
- The system randomly assigns you a fate (rich or poor)
- Your outcome changes depending on your fate + choice
- At the end, you see how your life played out
